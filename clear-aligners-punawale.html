Common Orthodontic Conditions We Treat

Clear aligners are clinically proven to correct a wide variety of dental and aesthetic alignment issues.

Teeth Overcrowding

When there isn't enough space in the jaw, teeth overlap and twist, making cleaning difficult. Aligners gently expand and reposition teeth into healthy alignment.

Gapped Teeth (Diastema)

Gaps between front or side teeth can impact speech and self-confidence. Aligners close gaps predictably, creating an even, uniform smile line.

Overbite & Deep Bite

When the upper front teeth overlap excessively over the lower teeth, causing uneven enamel wear. Aligners restore harmonious bite balance.

Underbite & Crossbite

When lower teeth sit forward of upper teeth, or upper teeth fit inside lower teeth upon closing. Custom biomechanical trays correct tooth trajectory.

Have questions about pricing, daily wear, or treatment duration? Here are straightforward answers.

How many hours a day do I need to wear clear aligners? +

You must wear your clear aligners for 20 to 22 hours per day to achieve predictable tooth movement. You can easily remove them for eating, drinking anything other than water, and daily brushing and flossing.

Are clear aligners really invisible? +

Yes. Clear aligners are custom-molded from medical-grade, BPA-free transparent polymers. They fit snugly against your teeth, making them virtually undetectable in social, academic, and workplace video calls or meetings.

How long does clear aligner treatment take? +

Most adult and teen treatments range from 6 to 14 months, depending on whether you are correcting minor gap closures or complex bite adjustments. You will typically switch to a new set of trays every 7 to 14 days.
