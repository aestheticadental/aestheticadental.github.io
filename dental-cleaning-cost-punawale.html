Clinical Facts

Debunking Common Dental Cleaning Myths

Misconceptions often delay timely care. Here is what modern dental science says about scaling:

Myth #1

"Scaling wears down your enamel and weakens teeth"

Fact: Ultrasonic scalers vibrate at microscopic frequencies to dislodge hard tartar. The rounded tip never scrapes, shaves, or scratches your natural enamel. Enamel is the hardest biological substance in the human body and cannot be damaged by water-cooled ultrasonic vibrations.

Myth #2

"Teeth become loose after a cleaning session"

Fact: When heavy tartar accumulates around teeth, it acts like a dirty, infected "cement" wall that masks bone loss and inflames gums. Once this hardened bacterial layer is cleared away, teeth may feel slightly unfamiliar for a few days while healthy gum tissue tightens back onto clean roots.

Myth #3

"Cleaning creates permanent gaps between teeth"

Fact: Scaling does not create gaps. Tartar buildup causes chronic gum recession, pushing the gums downward. When the yellowish tartar bridges are removed, the natural spaces previously filled with calcified bacteria become visible again. Leaving tartar untreated leads to irreversible bone destruction.

Myth #4

"Teeth cleaning causes permanent tooth sensitivity"

Fact: Mild, temporary sensitivity to cold water is completely normal for 24 to 48 hours following a thorough scaling session, as tooth roots adjust to being free of bacterial debris. We apply a protective desensitizing paste that resolves this within days.

Self-Assessment

Signs You Are Due for a Professional Dental Cleaning

If you notice any of the following symptoms, schedule a cleaning before minor plaque turns into gum disease:

🩸

Bleeding When Brushing or Flossing

Healthy gums never bleed. Bleeding upon gentle brushing is the primary indicator of gingivitis (early bacterial gum inflammation).

⚠️

Yellow or Brown Crust on Teeth

Hardened calculus that cannot be brushed away, especially behind the lower front incisors and near the gum margins.

😷

Persistent Bad Breath (Halitosis)

Volatile sulfur compounds produced by anaerobic bacteria trapped inside deep tartar pockets cannot be eliminated with mouthwash alone.

☕

Stubborn Tea, Coffee or Smoke Stains

Extrinsic tannins adhere to tooth enamel, creating dark yellow and brown discolorations that require clinical prophy polishing.

Cleaning FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions About Teeth Cleaning

Straightforward answers regarding costs, pain levels, and aftercare in Punawale.

How much does routine teeth cleaning cost in Punawale, Pune? +

At Aesthetica Dental Clinic in Punawale, professional ultrasonic teeth cleaning (scaling) starts from ₹1,000 for standard plaque and tartar removal. Scaling paired with enamel stain polishing ranges from ₹1,500 to ₹2,000. Deep subgingival cleaning for gum disease starts from ₹1,200 per quadrant.

Does teeth cleaning (scaling) damage tooth enamel or make teeth loose? +

No, this is a widespread dental myth. Modern scaling uses ultrasonic tips that gently vibrate micro-soundwaves and cool water spray to dislodge hardened tartar. It does not scrape, cut, or wear away your natural enamel. If teeth feel slightly spaced or different after cleaning, it is because hardened calculus that was inflaming your gums and pushing them down has been safely removed.

How often should I get my teeth professionally cleaned? +

Dental associations recommend professional scaling every 6 months. Patients with active orthodontic braces, clear aligners, smoking habits, or a history of bleeding gums and periodontitis benefit from cleaning every 3 to 4 months.

Does professional teeth cleaning hurt? +

Routine scaling is virtually painless and does not require local anesthesia. You may feel a slight vibrating sensation and cool water spray. If your gums are inflamed, sensitive, or require deep subgingival scaling, a mild numbing gel is applied to ensure complete comfort throughout the 30-minute session.

Will teeth cleaning make my teeth white? +

Teeth cleaning and polishing remove dark surface stains caused by tea, coffee, smoking, and turmeric, restoring your teeth to their clean, natural shade. However, if you desire teeth that are several shades lighter than your natural genetic color, you can opt for professional dental bleaching (teeth whitening) after scaling.
