Zero-Compromise 4-Tier Infection Control System

At Aesthetica Dental Clinic, patient safety is our highest clinical priority. Whether you are visiting for a routine teeth cleaning, a surgical dental implant, or root canal therapy, every instrument undergoes a rigorous multi-stage medical sterilization cycle meeting American Dental Association (ADA) and CDC standards.

1

Enzymatic Ultrasonic Cleansing

Instruments are immediately immersed in medical-grade multi-enzyme disinfectant baths and placed in ultrasonic scrubbers to detach microscopic debris, protein deposits, and bio-burden from dental tool surfaces.

2

Automated Pouch Sealing & Chemical Indicators

After thorough drying and inspection, clinical instruments are hermetically heat-sealed inside color-changing chemical indicator sterilization pouches that visually prove complete thermal disinfection.

3

Class-B Fractionated Vacuum Autoclave

Pouches are sterilized inside a European Class-B medical autoclave at 134°C under high atmospheric pressure. Triple vacuum pulsing eliminates trapped air, guaranteeing steam penetration through hollow dental handpieces.

4

Chairside Unsealing In Front of Patients

Sealed pouches remain unopened until you are seated in the operatory chair. Pouches are broken open right before your eyes, guaranteeing zero cross-contamination between patients.

Surface Disinfection & Operatory Sterilization

Between every single dental consultation and clinical appointment, our operatory undergoes a 10-minute turnaround protocol:

  • Hospital-Grade Surface Disinfection: Dental chairs, LED operatory lights, tray tables, and spittoons are wiped down with broad-spectrum hospital-grade surface disinfectants that eliminate 99.99% of bacteria, spores, and viruses.
  • Disposable Barrier Sleeves: Plastic barrier shields are replaced after every patient on light handles, digital intraoral sensors, air-water syringes, and suction tips.
  • Purified RO Waterlines: All dental delivery units operate on reverse-osmosis (RO) micro-filtered water lines treated with continuous antimicrobial tablets to prevent waterline biofilm buildup.
  • HEPA Air Filtration & UV Sanitization: Our treatment operatory is equipped with medical-grade air purification units and nightly germicidal UV-C room sterilization.

Why Sterilization Matters for NRIs & High-Risk Patients

Infection control is especially crucial for dental tourism visitors, elderly individuals, and patients with diabetic or cardiac conditions whose immune responses require a pristine surgical environment. Frequently Asked Questions on Clinic Hygiene

How do I know the dental tools are sterile?

Each instrument set is sealed inside a medical pouch featuring a pink-to-brown chemical steam indicator. Our dental assistant unseals this pouch directly in front of you once you are comfortably seated.

Are dental drill handpieces autoclaved after every patient?

Yes. Many clinics only surface-wipe rotary handpieces, but at Aesthetica Dental Clinic, every rotary handpiece undergoes high-pressure Class-B autoclaving between patient appointments.
