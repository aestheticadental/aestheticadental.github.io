Quick Answer: How Many Days Do You Need in Pune for Dental Work?

The required stay duration in Pune depends on your treatment type: (1) Routine scaling, fillings, or whitening: 1 day (same-day completion); (2) Single-sitting root canal (RCT): 1 to 2 days; (3) CAD/CAM Zirconia crown or bridge: 3 to 5 business days (prep on Day 1, permanent delivery on Day 4); (4) Porcelain veneers / smile makeover: 5 to 7 days; (5) Dental implants: 3 to 5 days for initial titanium fixture placement, followed by final crown placement 3 to 6 months later (or 7 to 10 days for qualifying immediate-load implants).

✈️ Essential Flying Safety: Why You Need Buffer Days

Commercial aircraft cabins are pressurized to altitudes of 6,000 to 8,000 feet. This creates subtle atmospheric pressure differentials that cause trapped microscopic air pockets to expand—a clinical phenomenon known as barodontalgia. Planning your stay duration with safe recovery buffers protects you against travel discomfort:

Routine Dentistry (Same Day) Cleanings, stain polishing, and bonded fillings have zero pressure risk. You can board a flight immediately after your visit.
Root Canals (24-Hour Buffer) Our rotary thermoplastic obturation hermetically seals root canals without voids, ensuring comfortable cabin pressure adaptation within 24 hours.
Implants & Extractions (48–72 Hrs) Allow 2 to 3 days for stable blood clot formation and tissue sealing before embarking on long-haul transcontinental flights.
Sinus Lifts (7–10 Days) If advanced bone grafting or a maxillary sinus lift is required, observe a 7 to 10-day recovery window to safeguard sinus membranes.

Frequently Asked Questions About Stay Durations

How many days should I stay in Pune for a root canal and dental crown? +

You should plan to stay between 4 to 6 days. At Aesthetica Dental Clinic, single-sitting rotary root canal treatment is performed in a single 45-minute visit on Day 1, followed immediately by digital 3D scans. Permanent CAD/CAM monolithic Zirconia crowns are milled and bonded by Day 4 or 5, leaving comfortable time for bite verification before flying.

How long do I need to stay in Pune for dental implants? +

For standard two-stage titanium implants, your initial visit requires 3 to 5 days in Pune for clinical assessment, 3D CBCT bone evaluation, implant fixture placement, and immediate temporary tooth fitting. Osseointegration then takes 3 to 6 months in your home country, followed by a second 3 to 5-day visit to Pune for final permanent Zirconia crown placement. Immediate-load implants can be completed in a single 7 to 10-day trip if bone density qualifies.

Can I leave Pune and travel around India while my crowns or veneers are being made? +

Yes. Once preliminary treatments and digital scans are completed, our digital lab takes 3 business days to precision-mill your custom restorations. During this window, you can visit family, explore Mumbai (90 mins away), or relax in hill stations like Lonavala and Khandala (40 mins away), wearing comfortable protective temporary crowns.

How soon after dental treatment is it safe to board an international flight? +

Routine cleanings, tooth-colored fillings, and crown cementations carry zero flight restrictions—you can fly the same day. For single-sitting root canals, we recommend a 24-hour buffer. For minor surgical extractions or dental implant fixture placement, waiting 48 to 72 hours before flying is essential to prevent cabin pressure changes from triggering discomfort or sinus irritation.

Is Aesthetica Dental Clinic open on weekends for traveling patients? +

Yes. Aesthetica Dental Clinic is open all 7 days a week, including Saturdays and Sundays, from 10:15 AM to 8:30 PM. This allows visiting travelers and NRIs to schedule appointments over the weekend without disrupting weekday plans or domestic travel schedules.
