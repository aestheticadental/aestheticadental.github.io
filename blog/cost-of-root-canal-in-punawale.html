Root Canal Treatment Cost in Punawale — Quick Answer

Root canal treatment at Aesthetica Dental Clinic, Punawale costs between ₹3,500 and ₹8,000 depending on which tooth is being treated and how many root canals it has. A crown is additionally recommended after treatment.

If you've been told you need a root canal and you're wondering what it will cost in Punawale, this guide covers everything you need to know — including a full pricing table, what's included in the cost, what's charged separately, and what factors affect your bill.

Root Canal Cost by Tooth Type

The single biggest factor affecting root canal cost is which tooth needs treatment. This is because different teeth have different numbers of root canals, and more canals mean more time and materials.

Tooth TypeNumber of CanalsTypical Cost at Aesthetica
Front teeth (incisors & canines)1 canal₹3,500 – ₹4,500
Premolars1–2 canals₹4,500 – ₹6,000
Molars (back teeth)3–4 canals₹6,000 – ₹8,000

What Is Included in the Root Canal Fee?

At Aesthetica Dental Clinic, the root canal treatment fee includes:

  • Local anaesthesia (so you feel no pain during the procedure)
  • Digital X-ray for diagnosis and treatment planning
  • Removal of infected pulp tissue using rotary endodontic instruments
  • Canal cleaning, shaping and irrigation
  • Permanent filling and sealing of the root canals
  • Temporary or permanent tooth restoration

What Is Charged Separately?

  • Crown after root canal: ₹5,000 – ₹15,000 — A crown is strongly recommended after RCT (especially for back teeth) to protect the treated tooth. Crown cost varies by material: ceramic, zirconia, or PFM.
  • Initial consultation: ₹200 – ₹500 — This is typically adjusted against the treatment cost if you proceed.
  • OPG X-ray (if needed): ₹500 – ₹800 — A full jaw X-ray may be needed in complex cases.

💡 Tip: Always ask for a written cost estimate covering the RCT fee AND the crown before treatment begins. At Aesthetica, we provide a complete written breakdown at the consultation — no surprises.

Factors That Affect Root Canal Cost in Punawale

Beyond tooth type, several other factors can affect the final cost:

  • Severity of infection: Heavily infected teeth or those with curved/calcified canals may require more time and specialist techniques.
  • Number of sittings: Most RCTs at Aesthetica are completed in 1–2 sittings. Severe infections may require an interim dressing between appointments.
  • Crown material: Ceramic and zirconia crowns cost more than PFM (porcelain-fused-to-metal) but look more natural and are stronger.
  • Technology used: Clinics using rotary endodontics and digital X-rays (as at Aesthetica) may charge slightly more than clinics using older manual techniques — but the treatment is faster, more precise, and more comfortable.

Is Root Canal Cheaper Than Extraction + Implant?

Many patients ask whether they should "just pull the tooth." Here's the honest comparison:

  • Root canal + crown: ₹8,500 – ₹23,000 (one-time, preserves your natural tooth)
  • Extraction + dental implant + crown: ₹30,000 – ₹50,000 (higher upfront, plus 3–6 month healing period)
  • Extraction + bridge: ₹15,000 – ₹30,000 (requires grinding down adjacent healthy teeth)

In most cases, saving your natural tooth with a root canal is the most cost-effective and clinically superior option. Natural teeth are always preferable to artificial replacements.

Does Insurance Cover Root Canal Treatment in Pune?

Some health insurance policies in India cover dental procedures including root canal treatment. Check with your insurer whether dental is included in your policy. Most group health policies through employers do not cover routine dental but may cover dental emergencies. Aesthetica provides an itemised receipt that you can submit for reimbursement claims.

Is EMI Available for Root Canal Treatment?

Yes. Aesthetica Dental Clinic accepts Credit Cards and Debit Cards — and EMI can be arranged on select high-value treatments through your card provider. Ask at the clinic for details at the time of treatment.

Root Canal Cost — FAQs

What is the cost of root canal treatment in Punawale?

Root canal treatment at Aesthetica Dental Clinic, Punawale costs ₹3,500 – ₹8,000 depending on tooth type. Front teeth (1 canal): ₹3,500–₹4,500. Premolars: ₹4,500–₹6,000. Molars (3–4 canals): ₹6,000–₹8,000. Crown is charged separately.

Does the root canal cost include the crown?

No. The root canal fee (₹3,500–₹8,000) covers the procedure itself. A crown is additionally recommended and costs ₹5,000–₹15,000 depending on material. We provide a combined estimate at your consultation.

Is root canal treatment painful?

At Aesthetica, root canal treatment is performed under local anaesthesia and is virtually painless. Most patients say it's more comfortable than a tooth extraction. Mild soreness for 1–2 days afterward is normal.

How many sittings is root canal in Punawale?

Most root canals at Aesthetica are completed in 1–2 sittings of 45–60 minutes each. Single-sitting root canals are possible for straightforward cases. Complex infections may require 2 visits.
