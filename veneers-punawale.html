Veneers vs Crowns — Which Do You Need?

Both restore the appearance and function of teeth — the right choice depends on how much of the tooth is damaged and your aesthetic goals.

💎

Porcelain Veneers

Ultra-thin porcelain shells bonded to the front surface of teeth. Require minimal tooth preparation (0.3–0.5mm enamel removal). Ideal for cosmetic corrections — chips, cracks, stains, gaps, and slightly misaligned teeth. Provide a dramatic smile transformation with a natural finish.

👑

Composite Veneers

Made from tooth-coloured composite resin and bonded directly to the tooth in a single visit. More affordable than porcelain veneers and require no enamel removal in some cases. Ideal for minor cosmetic corrections. Less durable than porcelain but repairable if chipped.

🏆

Dental Crowns (Caps)

Cover the entire tooth — used when a tooth is severely damaged, decayed, or has undergone root canal treatment. Restore full strength, shape and appearance. Available in ceramic, zirconia, and PFM materials. Digital shade-matching ensures a natural-looking result.

What Can Veneers Correct?

Veneers are one of the most versatile cosmetic dental solutions — addressing multiple concerns in a single treatment.

🪨
Chipped or Cracked Teeth
Minor chips and cracks that affect the appearance of front teeth can be completely concealed with a precisely fitted veneer.
🟡
Permanent Staining or Discolouration
Tetracycline stains, fluorosis, and staining that doesn't respond to whitening are effectively masked by porcelain veneers.
🔲
Gaps Between Teeth (Diastema)
Small gaps between front teeth can be closed with veneers without the need for braces or aligners — ideal for patients wanting instant results.
📐
Slightly Misaligned or Uneven Teeth
Mild crowding or unevenness can be visually corrected with veneers — sometimes called "instant orthodontics" for cases where braces are overkill.
📏
Short or Worn-Down Teeth
Teeth worn down by grinding (bruxism) or naturally short teeth can be lengthened and shaped with veneers to create better proportion and symmetry.
✨
Full Smile Makeover
A complete transformation of your smile — 6 to 10 veneers across the visible front teeth — is the most dramatic cosmetic dental change possible without orthodontics.

Veneer Placement Process at Aesthetica

A precise, artistic process completed in 2–3 appointments.

1
Smile Consultation

Dr. Prachi reviews your smile goals, assesses your teeth and bite, and recommends the most suitable option — veneers, crowns, or a combination — with a cost estimate.

2
Digital Shade Selection

Your desired shade is selected using a digital shade guide to ensure veneers blend naturally with your existing teeth or achieve the bright white look you want.

3
Tooth Preparation

A thin layer of enamel (0.3–0.5mm) is removed from the tooth surface to create space for the veneer. Local anaesthesia is used for comfort. Impressions are taken.

4
Temporary Veneers

Temporary veneers are placed while your custom porcelain veneers are fabricated in the dental laboratory — typically 1–2 weeks.

5
Trial Fitting

The finished veneers are placed without bonding first for a trial fit — colour, shape and bite are checked. Adjustments are made if needed before permanent bonding.

6
Permanent Bonding

Veneers are permanently bonded to teeth with dental adhesive and cured with a UV light. Final polishing gives your new smile its natural brilliance.

Veneers & Crowns FAQs

How much do veneers cost in Punawale?

Porcelain veneers at Aesthetica cost ₹8,000 – ₹15,000 per tooth. Composite veneers start from ₹4,000 per tooth. Full smile makeover packages (6–8 teeth) are quoted at consultation. A detailed cost breakdown is always provided before treatment begins.

How long do veneers last?

Porcelain veneers last 10–15 years on average with good oral hygiene. Composite veneers last 5–7 years. Longevity depends on oral hygiene, diet, and whether you grind your teeth at night (a night guard is recommended if you do).

Are veneers painful to get?

Local anaesthesia is used during the preparation stage, so you won't feel pain. Some mild sensitivity for a few days after preparation is normal and subsides once the permanent veneers are bonded.

What is the difference between a veneer and a crown?

A veneer covers only the front surface of the tooth — minimal preparation required, primarily cosmetic. A crown covers the entire tooth and is used for significantly damaged, weakened or root-canal-treated teeth. Dr. Prachi will recommend the right option after examining your teeth.

Can veneers fix crooked teeth?

Veneers can correct the appearance of mildly misaligned or uneven teeth without orthodontic treatment — sometimes called "instant orthodontics." For moderate to severe crowding, aligners or braces are a better long-term solution. Dr. Prachi will advise the right approach at consultation.

How many teeth can be treated with veneers in one go?

A full smile makeover typically involves 6–10 veneers covering all teeth visible when you smile. Single-tooth corrections are also done. The number depends on your smile and cosmetic goals — discussed thoroughly at your smile consultation.
