Root Canal Aftercare Instructions & Recovery

Step-by-step recovery guidelines, diet recommendations, medication management, and protection tips following your root canal treatment with Dr. Prachi Gupta Garg.

Ask Dr. Prachi on WhatsApp

Immediate 24-Hour Precautions

  • Wait for Numbness to Wear Off: Do not eat hot meals or chew solid foods until your lip, cheek, and tongue are fully sensitive (usually 2 to 3 hours). This prevents accidental severe cheek biting.
  • Chew on the Opposite Side: Until your permanent crown is placed, avoid chewing hard foods (nuts, raw carrots, ice) on the treated tooth to prevent fracturing the weakened enamel walls.
  • Take Prescribed Medications Promptly: Take your prescribed anti-inflammatory or pain reliever before the local anesthesia completely wears off.

Recovery Milestone Timeline

Day 1 – 2

Mild Tenderness When Biting

The ligament around the tooth root tip is inflamed from instrumentation. This is completely normal and responds well to prescribed analgesics.

Day 3 – 5

Steadily Decreasing Sensitivity

Biting tenderness subsides. Continue normal brushing and gentle flossing around the tooth. If the temporary filling feels slightly gritty or chalky, this is normal.

Week 1 – 2

Permanent Crown Impression

Return to Aesthetica Dental Clinic for the digital or rubber base impression for your customized dental crown (Zirconia, E-max, or PFM) to permanently seal and protect the tooth.

Dietary Guidelines During Healing

Recommended Soft Foods

Khichdi, curd rice, dal, scrambled eggs, mashed potatoes, soft pasta, smoothies, and warm soups at room temperature.

Foods to Avoid

Sticky caramels, chewing gum, hard nuts, crusty bread, popcorn, and boiling hot beverages that could dislodge the temporary filling.

Warning Signs Requiring Immediate Attention:

  • Visible swelling in your cheek, jaw, or gums.
  • Allergic rash, itching, or breathing difficulty from prescribed antibiotics.
  • The temporary filling completely falls out, exposing the canal chamber.
  • Persistent severe throbbing that does not respond to painkillers after day 3.

Have unexpected discomfort? Review our guide on why teeth hurt after root canals or contact our emergency desk.
