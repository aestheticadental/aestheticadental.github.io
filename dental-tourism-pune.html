International FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions by Traveling Patients

Everything you need to know about planning your dental visit to Pune.

How much can I save on dental treatments in Pune, India compared to the US or UK? +

International patients and NRIs typically save between 70% and 80% on major dental treatments in India without compromising clinical quality. For example, a single titanium dental implant with a custom zirconia crown that costs $3,000 to $5,000 in the US or £2,500 in the UK costs approximately ₹30,000 to ₹45,000 ($360 to $540 USD) at Aesthetica Dental Clinic.

How do I travel to Aesthetica Dental Clinic from Mumbai International Airport (BOM)? +

Aesthetica Dental Clinic is strategically situated in Punawale on Kate Wasti Road, immediately adjacent to the Mumbai-Pune Expressway exit. International travelers arriving at Mumbai Airport (Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport - BOM) can take an expressway taxi directly to our clinic in just 90 to 120 minutes, bypassing downtown Pune city traffic entirely.

How many days should I plan for my dental treatment trip? +

Treatment timelines depend on the procedure. Single-sitting root canals, cleanings, and composite restorations require 1 to 2 days. CAD/CAM monolithic Zirconia crowns or aesthetic veneers require 3 to 5 business days for precision custom milling. For standard dental implants, an initial 3 to 5-day visit is needed for fixture placement, followed by final crown fitting 3 to 6 months later, or immediate-load temporary restorations during a single trip.

Are the implant and restorative materials FDA or CE certified? +

Yes. We exclusively use internationally recognized, FDA-approved and CE-certified implant systems, biocompatible titanium abutments, and certified monolithic Zirconia and E-max ceramic materials manufactured by global dental leaders.

Can I receive a treatment plan and cost estimate before booking my flight? +

Yes. We provide a complimentary pre-arrival virtual consultation. Simply send your recent dental X-rays (IOPA or OPG), CBCT scans, or smartphone photos of your smile via WhatsApp (+91 92266 80164). Dr. Prachi Gupta Garg will review your case and provide an itemized treatment proposal, estimated timeline, and exact pricing before you confirm travel.
