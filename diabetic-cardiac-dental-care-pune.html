Why Systemic Health Requires Specialized Dental Care

Your oral health is directly intertwined with your cardiovascular and endocrine systems. Uncontrolled blood sugar impairs collagen synthesis and wound healing, while heart conditions necessitate strict infection control to avoid bacterial endocarditis. At Aesthetica Dental Clinic, Dr. Prachi Gupta Garg adheres to comprehensive clinical safety protocols before performing any invasive treatment.

Type 2 Diabetes & HbA1c Management

We review recent HbA1c values prior to surgical extractions or dental implants. Controlled diabetic levels (HbA1c ≤ 7.5%) allow normal dental healing with antibiotic coverage. Learn more in our guide on diabetic dental implants.

Cardiovascular & Hypertension Safety

Every patient's blood pressure is measured prior to administering local anesthesia. When indicated, we use plain (epinephrine-free) anesthetic agents like Mepivacaine to avoid cardiac arrhythmias or blood pressure spikes.

Blood Thinners (Anticoagulants) Protocol

Patients taking Aspirin, Clopidogrel (Plavix), or Warfarin receive specialized local hemostatic measures (gelatin sponges, suturing, tranexamic acid gauze) without discontinuing life-saving cardiac medications without physician consent.

Our Safe Clinical Protocol for Medical Consultations

  1. Medical History Documentation: Complete review of prescription drugs, dosages, cardiac stents, pacemakers, and allergies.
  2. Morning Appointments: Scheduling early morning slots when endogenous cortisol levels are optimal and blood glucose is stable post-breakfast.
  3. Physician Clearance: Direct telephonic or written coordination with your primary cardiologist or endocrinologist when required.
  4. Stress-Reduction Protocol: Short, gentle appointments designed to minimize dental anxiety and adrenaline surges.
  5. Strict Post-Op Follow-up: Monitored recovery protocols and tailored prescriptions for safe pain management.

Infection Control & Endocarditis Prevention

Certain cardiac conditions—such as prosthetic heart valves, history of infective endocarditis, or congenital heart defects—require premedication with prophylactic antibiotics 1 hour prior to procedures that manipulate gingival tissue. Combined with our hospital-grade sterilization standards, your treatment is completely safeguarded.
