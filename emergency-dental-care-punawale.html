Urgent Dental Care

Common Dental Emergencies We Treat Immediately

If you are experiencing any of the following symptoms, visit our Punawale clinic straight away to prevent permanent nerve damage or spreading infections.

⚡

Severe, Throbbing Toothache

Intense, radiating pain that keeps you awake at night or worsens with hot/cold drinks typically indicates an infected nerve (acute pulpitis). We provide immediate relief and single-visit root canal options.

Immediate Treatment: Anesthesia & RCT →
🔨

Broken, Chipped or Cracked Tooth

Trauma from a fall, sports injury, or biting down on hard food can expose the sensitive inner dentin or pulp. We perform composite bonding, dental splinting, or same-day protective caps.

Immediate Treatment: Bonding & Crown Cap →
🦷

Knocked-Out (Avulsed) Tooth

A completely dislodged permanent tooth can often be saved and re-implanted if treated within 30 to 60 minutes. Keep it moist in milk and reach our clinic immediately.

Immediate Treatment: Re-implantation & Splinting →
⚠️

Facial Swelling & Dental Abscess

Pus buildup around the root or gum line causes tender facial swelling and fever. An abscess requires prompt clinical drainage and targeted antibiotics to prevent the infection from spreading.

Immediate Treatment: Drainage & Antibiotic Therapy →
🤕

Impacted Wisdom Tooth Pain

Food entrapment beneath an inflamed gum flap (pericoronitis) around a wisdom tooth causes severe jaw stiffness and localized swelling. We perform gentle irrigation and same-day extractions.

Immediate Treatment: Operculectomy or Extraction →
🩹

Lost Filling or Dislodged Crown

A missing restoration leaves the underlying tooth exposed to air, temperature, and bacteria. We recement loose crowns or place durable tooth-colored composite restorations.

Immediate Treatment: Recementation & Sealing →
Emergency First-Aid Protocol

What to Do Right Now Before Reaching the Clinic

Follow these dentist-approved first-aid steps to protect your tooth and manage acute discomfort on your way to Swaraaj Heights.

Immediate Action Checklist:

Do NOT place aspirin directly on the gums or tooth, as this causes chemical tissue burns.

1

Warm Saltwater Rinse

Gently rinse your mouth with lukewarm salt water to clear away dislodged food debris and soothe inflamed gum tissues.

2

Cold Ice Compress

Apply an ice pack wrapped in a cloth to the outside of your cheek for 10 minutes to reduce facial swelling and numb sharp pain.

3

Preserve Dislodged Teeth

If a tooth has fallen out, hold it by the crown only. Store it in cold milk or saline solution. Do not scrape or dry the root.

4

What should I do if a permanent tooth is knocked out completely? +

Handle the tooth only by the crown (top), never touch the root. Gently rinse it with milk or clean water without scrubbing. If possible, gently place the tooth back into its socket. If not, store it in a container of cold milk or your own saliva and reach our clinic within 30 to 60 minutes for the highest chance of re-implantation.
