Tooth Extraction Aftercare & Recovery Guide

Essential instructions to protect your blood clot, prevent dry socket (alveolar osteitis), and ensure rapid, comfortable healing after tooth removal.

Ask Dr. Prachi on WhatsApp

The Critical First 24 Hours: Protecting the Blood Clot

The most important phase of extraction recovery is the formation and stabilization of the blood clot inside the bone socket. This clot protects underlying nerve endings and serves as the biological scaffold for new bone growth.

Bite Firmly on the Gauze Pack

Keep firm, continuous pressure on the sterile gauze placed over the extraction site for 45 to 60 minutes. If mild oozing continues, replace with fresh clean gauze or a damp black tea bag for another 30 minutes.

Do NOT Spit or Use Drinking Straws

Suction pressure inside your mouth will dislodge the delicate blood clot, exposing bare bone. Swallow your saliva naturally instead of spitting forcefully.

Zero Smoking, Tobacco or Alcohol

Nicotine constricts peripheral blood vessels, drastically impairing healing, while chemical toxins dissolve the clot, causing excruciating dry socket.

Step-by-Step Day-by-Day Timeline

Hours 1 – 24

Cold Compress & Rest

Apply an ice pack to the outside of your cheek (15 mins on, 15 mins off) to minimize swelling. Keep your head elevated with an extra pillow when resting. Eat cold, soft foods (ice cream, yogurt, cold milk).

Day 2 – 7

Warm Saltwater Rinses

Starting 24 hours post-procedure, gently bathe the area with warm saltwater (half teaspoon salt in a glass of warm water) 3 to 4 times daily after meals. Do not rinse vigorously.

Day 7 – 10

Suture Removal & Healing Review

If non-resorbable sutures were placed (common in surgical wisdom tooth extractions), visit Aesthetica Dental Clinic for painless suture removal.

What Is Dry Socket & How to Prevent It?

Dry socket (alveolar osteitis) occurs when the blood clot is dislodged prematurely, exposing alveolar bone and nerve endings to air, saliva, and food particles. It typically manifests 3 to 5 days after extraction as a throbbing, radiating ache towards the ear.

If you experience symptoms of dry socket, visit us immediately. Dr. Prachi will gently irrigate the socket and apply a soothing sedative clove oil dressing (Alveogyl) for instant pain relief.

Call the Clinic Immediately If:

  • Active bleeding continues after 4 hours of continuous firm gauze pressure.
  • Severe pain starts or worsens on day 3 or 4.
  • You develop a fever exceeding 100.4°F (38°C) or noticeable difficulty swallowing.
