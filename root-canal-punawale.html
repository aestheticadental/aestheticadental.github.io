What Is Root Canal Treatment?

A tooth-saving procedure that removes infection from inside your tooth — relieving pain and preventing extraction.

🦷

Saves Your Natural Tooth

Root canal treatment removes infected pulp tissue from inside the tooth, allowing you to keep your natural tooth rather than having it extracted. A natural tooth is always better than an artificial replacement.

💉

Completely Painless at Aesthetica

We use advanced local anaesthesia techniques before the procedure begins, ensuring you feel nothing during treatment. Rotary endodontic instruments make the process faster and more comfortable than traditional methods.

✅

Long-Lasting Results

A properly treated and crowned tooth can last a lifetime with normal oral hygiene. Root canal treatment has a 95%+ success rate when followed by a quality crown and regular check-ups.

Do You Need Root Canal Treatment?

Watch for these signs — early treatment prevents tooth loss and more complex procedures.

😣
Severe, persistent toothache
Pain when chewing or biting, or throbbing pain that doesn't subside on its own.
🌡️
Prolonged sensitivity to hot or cold
Sensitivity that lingers more than a few seconds after the hot or cold stimulus is removed.
🫧
Swollen or tender gums
Pimple-like abscess on the gums near the tooth, or visible swelling around the jaw.
🌑
Tooth darkening or discolouration
A tooth turning grey or dark often indicates the nerve inside has died and infection has set in.
🦷
Deep decay or cracked tooth
Decay or a crack that has reached the pulp chamber requires root canal treatment to save the tooth.

Root Canal Treatment Process at Aesthetica

A clear, step-by-step procedure designed for your comfort — from diagnosis to restoration.

1
Consultation & Digital X-Ray

Dr. Prachi examines the tooth and takes a digital OPG X-ray to assess the extent of infection and plan treatment precisely.

2
Local Anaesthesia

The area is completely numbed before any instrument touches the tooth. You'll feel pressure — but no pain.

3
Pulp Removal & Canal Cleaning

Rotary endodontic instruments remove infected pulp tissue and clean all root canals to eliminate bacteria.

4
Shaping & Irrigation

Canals are shaped and irrigated with antiseptic solution to ensure complete disinfection of the root system.

5
Filling & Sealing

Canals are filled with biocompatible gutta-percha and permanently sealed to prevent re-infection.

6
Crown Placement

A dental crown is placed over the treated tooth to restore full strength, function and natural appearance.

Root Canal FAQs

Answers to the most common questions patients ask before their treatment.

Is root canal treatment painful?

At Aesthetica, root canal treatment is performed under local anaesthesia and is virtually painless. Most patients report it's more comfortable than a tooth extraction. Mild soreness for 1–2 days afterward is normal and manageable with over-the-counter pain relief.

How much does root canal treatment cost in Punawale?

Root canal treatment at Aesthetica costs between ₹3,500 and ₹8,000 depending on which tooth is being treated and how many canals it has. Front teeth are simpler and cost less; molars with 3–4 canals cost more. A crown is additionally recommended after treatment.

How many sittings does root canal treatment take?

Most root canals at Aesthetica are completed in 1–2 appointments of 45–60 minutes each. Single-sitting root canals are possible for straightforward cases. More complex cases or teeth with severe infection may require 2 visits.

Do I need a crown after root canal treatment?

Yes — a crown is strongly recommended after root canal treatment, especially on back teeth. The procedure removes the tooth's nerve and blood supply, making it more brittle over time. A crown restores full strength and protects it from cracking.

What happens if I don't get root canal treatment?

Untreated dental infections can spread to the jawbone and surrounding teeth, cause a painful abscess, and ultimately lead to tooth loss. Delaying treatment makes the problem more complex and expensive to fix.
