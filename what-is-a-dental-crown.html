Quick Answer: What is a Dental Crown and Why is It Used?

A dental crown (commonly called a tooth cap) is a custom-fabricated, tooth-shaped restoration that encloses the entire visible portion of a compromised tooth above the gum line. Its primary medical purpose is to restore structural strength, anatomical shape, and bite alignment to teeth that are fractured, severely decayed, restored with large fillings, or hollowed out after a root canal. Crowns are crafted from biocompatible materials such as monolithic Zirconia, lithium disilicate (E-Max), or Porcelain-Fused-to-Metal (PFM).

Clinical Indications

When Do You Need a Dental Crown?

Dr. Prachi Gupta Garg recommends crowns when natural tooth structure is too weakened for a simple composite filling.

🛡️

After a Root Canal (RCT)

A root canal saves your tooth from internal infection, but leaves the enamel dry and brittle without blood flow. A crown prevents the tooth from cracking under normal chewing pressure.

⚡

Cracked or Fractured Tooth

Teeth damaged by chewing hard foods, trauma, or nighttime bruxism need a 360-degree crown to hold the tooth together and stop fracture lines from reaching the root.

🦷

Extensive Cavities & Fillings

When decay destroys more than 50% of the natural tooth, standard fillings cannot support chewing loads. A crown rebuilds the full anatomical cusps reliably.

🔩

Topping a Dental Implant

Once a titanium dental implant integrates into the jawbone, a custom ceramic crown is attached to the abutment, recreating a permanent, natural-looking replacement tooth.

Clinical Workflow

The Dental Crown Procedure: Step-by-Step

Getting a custom crown at Aesthetica Dental Clinic is smooth, predictable, and completely painless.

1

Clinical Exam & Digital X-Ray

Dr. Prachi evaluates the tooth's root health using low-radiation digital RVG radiography. Any residual decay is cleared and a strong core buildup is placed.

2

Conservative Preparation

Under gentle local anesthesia, the tooth is minimally shaped along the sides and top by 1 to 1.5 mm to create space for the crown to seat flush with neighboring teeth.

3

Digital Impression & Temp Cap

An impression is recorded and sent to our partner digital dental laboratory. A comfortable temporary crown is placed immediately to protect your tooth while you wait.

4

Final Check & Cementation

In your second visit (3–5 days later), the custom crown is checked for shade match, margins, and bite balance, then permanently bonded using medical-grade adhesive resin.

Long-Term Maintenance

How to Make Your Dental Crown Last 15+ Years

While crowns cannot get cavities, the underlying natural tooth root and surrounding gums still require care.

🪥 Brush & Floss Daily

Pay special attention to the gumline margin where the crown meets the tooth root to prevent bacterial plaque accumulation.

🧊 Avoid Chewing Ice or Bones

Never use your crowned teeth as tools to tear open packaging, crack hard nut shells, or bite into ice cubes.

😴 Wear a Night Guard if Grinding

If you clench your teeth during sleep (bruxism), a custom night guard shields ceramic crowns from extreme nighttime bite pressures.

🗓️ Routine 6-Month Checkups

Bi-annual scaling and professional checkups ensure the cement seal remains intact and surrounding gums stay healthy.

Patient FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Crowns

Straightforward answers to the most common questions about dental caps in Punawale.

Why is a dental crown strictly necessary after a Root Canal Treatment (RCT)? +

During an RCT, infected pulp, nerves, and blood vessels are cleaned out from inside the tooth. Without a blood supply, the tooth loses moisture and becomes brittle over time. Heavy chewing forces can split an unprotected tooth down to the root, requiring an emergency extraction. A dental crown encloses the tooth in a 360-degree protective shield, preventing fractures and restoring full chewing strength. Read our clinical guide comparing crown types & costs after RCT →

What is the difference between a Zirconia crown and a PFM crown? +

PFM (Porcelain-Fused-to-Metal) crowns have an inner metal alloy substructure coated with dental porcelain. While strong, they can chip and may reveal a dark grey metal margin near the gum line over time. Zirconia crowns are 100% metal-free, CAD/CAM milled from monolithic zirconium dioxide. They offer superior fracture resistance, natural translucency, biocompatibility, and will never show a dark gumline.

At Aesthetica Dental Clinic in Punawale, standard Porcelain-Fused-to-Metal (PFM) crowns start from ₹3,000. Monolithic CAD/CAM Zirconia crowns range from ₹6,500 to ₹10,000, while premium layered aesthetic Zirconia and E-max ceramic crowns for front teeth start from ₹10,000 with long warranty terms.

How long does a dental crown last? +

With diligent oral hygiene (daily brushing, flossing, and 6-month dental checkups), high-quality dental crowns typically last between 10 to 15 years, and premium Zirconia crowns can often last 20+ years or a lifetime.

Does getting a dental crown hurt? +

No. The tooth preparation procedure is performed under gentle local anesthesia. If the tooth has already undergone a root canal, the tooth nerve is gone, so the procedure is completely painless. You may only experience mild gum tenderness for 24 to 48 hours afterward.
