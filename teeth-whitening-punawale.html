What Types of Stains Does Whitening Treat?

Professional whitening is highly effective for extrinsic (surface) stains and mild intrinsic discolouration.

☕
Tea, Coffee & Chai Stains
Daily consumption of tea and coffee is the most common cause of yellowing in Indian patients. Professional whitening removes these tannin-based stains effectively.
🚬
Tobacco & Smoking Stains
Nicotine and tar cause stubborn yellow-brown staining. Whitening can significantly lighten these — though stopping tobacco use is recommended to maintain results.
🍷
Food & Beverage Stains
Red wine, dark juices, curries and certain spices all contribute to surface staining over time. Professional whitening removes years of accumulated discolouration.
🕰️
Age-Related Yellowing
Enamel naturally thins with age, making the yellow dentine beneath more visible. Whitening brightens the enamel layer to counteract this gradual yellowing.
💊
Medication-Related Staining
Certain medications (like tetracycline taken in childhood) cause intrinsic staining that whitening may partially address — though severe cases may be better treated with veneers.

Teeth Whitening Process at Aesthetica

A safe, comfortable, in-clinic procedure from start to finish — completed in one appointment.

1
Pre-Treatment Assessment

Dr. Prachi examines your teeth, checks for cavities or gum disease (these must be treated first), and assesses your enamel to determine suitability and the best whitening protocol for you.

2
Shade Recording

Your current tooth shade is recorded on a standard shade guide. This is your before measurement — used to compare results after the procedure.

3
Gum Protection

A protective barrier is applied to your gums and soft tissue before any whitening agent is placed on your teeth — ensuring no irritation or sensitivity to the gums.

4
Whitening Agent Application

The professional whitening gel is carefully applied to all visible tooth surfaces. It is left to work for the prescribed time and may be activated with a light source for enhanced results.

5
Rinse & Repeat

The gel is rinsed off, and if required, a second application is done in the same session to achieve the desired shade level.

6
Final Shade Comparison

Your new shade is recorded and compared to the before measurement. Most patients achieve 4–8 shades lighter. Aftercare instructions are provided.

Teeth Whitening FAQs

Common questions answered by Dr. Prachi Gupta Garg.

How much does teeth whitening cost in Punawale?

Professional in-clinic teeth whitening at Aesthetica Dental Clinic, Punawale costs ₹4,000 – ₹8,000 per session depending on the degree of staining and the whitening system used. Take-home kits are also available from ₹3,000.

Is professional teeth whitening painful?

Most patients experience no pain during whitening. Some may feel mild temporary sensitivity during or after the procedure — especially those with pre-existing sensitivity. Dr. Prachi uses desensitising agents and monitors throughout to keep you comfortable.

How many shades whiter will my teeth get?

Results vary by individual, but most patients at Aesthetica achieve 4–8 shades lighter in a single session. The degree of improvement depends on the original shade, type of staining, and enamel thickness. Dr. Prachi will give you a realistic expectation at your consultation.

How long does teeth whitening last?

Professional whitening results typically last 1–3 years. Longevity depends on your diet (limiting tea, coffee, tobacco helps), oral hygiene, and whether you use a maintenance kit at home. Annual top-up sessions are available at a reduced cost.

Does teeth whitening work on crowns, veneers or fillings?

No. Whitening agents only lighten natural tooth enamel. Crowns, veneers and composite fillings will not change colour. If you have visible dental restorations, Dr. Prachi will advise on the best approach — which may include replacing restorations after whitening to match the new shade.

Who is NOT suitable for teeth whitening?

Whitening is not recommended for pregnant or breastfeeding patients, children under 16, patients with active cavities or gum disease (these must be treated first), or those with severe enamel erosion. Dr. Prachi assesses suitability at consultation.

Should I get scaling done before teeth whitening?

Yes — a professional scale and polish before whitening removes plaque and surface deposits, allowing the whitening agent to work directly on clean enamel and produce more even, effective results. Dr. Prachi may recommend this as a preparatory step.

Is Aesthetica open on weekends for teeth whitening?

Yes. Aesthetica Dental Clinic is open 7 days a week — Monday to Sunday, 10:15 AM to 8:30 PM — including public holidays. Weekend and evening whitening appointments are available.
