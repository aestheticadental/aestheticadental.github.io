Patient FAQs & Clinical Transparency

Clear, straightforward answers about treatments, scheduling and clinic facilities in Punawale.

Does Aesthetica Dental Clinic accept walk-ins for severe toothaches or emergency dental care?

Yes, we welcome same-day walk-ins and urgent consultations for dental emergencies, including acute toothache, broken or knocked-out teeth, lost fillings and sudden facial swellings. Our clinic is open 7 days a week from 10:15 AM to 8:30 PM at Swaraaj Heights, Kate Wasti Road, Punawale. Call 092266 80164 for immediate assistance.

Do you provide single-sitting root canal treatment (RCT)? Is the procedure painful?

Yes, we specialize in advanced, painless single-sitting Root Canal Treatments (RCT). Dr. Prachi Gupta Garg uses modern rotary endodontics, digital apex locators and low-radiation digital X-rays for precise treatment planning. Whether one sitting is appropriate depends on the clinical condition of the tooth.

Is Aesthetica Dental Clinic open on Sundays and late evenings?

Yes. Aesthetica Dental Clinic is open all 7 days of the week, including Sundays, from 10:15 AM to 8:30 PM.

What orthodontic and cosmetic smile treatments do you offer for adults and children?

We offer traditional metal braces, discreet ceramic braces, custom clear aligners, pediatric dental care, professional teeth whitening and custom porcelain veneers.

Where is the clinic located, and is there dedicated parking available?

Aesthetica Dental Clinic is located on the 1st Floor (Shop No. 103) of Swaraaj Heights on Kate Wasti Road, directly opposite Legacy Ivy in Punawale, Pune (411033). The premises offer free on-site parking, elevator access and wheelchair accessibility.
