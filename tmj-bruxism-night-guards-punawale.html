Are You Grinding Your Teeth at Night?

Teeth grinding (known clinically as bruxism) and Temporomandibular Joint (TMJ) dysfunction are increasingly common among software engineers, tech professionals, and busy residents across Hinjawadi, Wakad, and Punawale. Prolonged screen time, mental stress, and postural strain cause involuntary clenching of the masseter jaw muscles during sleep.

Common Warning Signs of Bruxism & TMJ Strain:

  • Dull Morning Headaches: Waking up with tension around your temples or forehead.
  • Jaw Joint Clicking or Popping: Noticeable clicking when opening wide, yawning, or chewing.
  • Enamel Wear & Flattened Teeth: Enamel chipping, flattened biting edges, or exposed yellow dentin.
  • Unexplained Tooth Sensitivity: Generalized sensitivity to cold water without visible cavities.
  • Neck & Shoulder Stiffness: Chronic tightness running down from the angle of the jaw into the trapezius.

Frequently Asked Questions on Night Guards

How long does a custom dental night guard last?

A custom dual-laminate night guard typically lasts between 3 to 5 years depending on the severity of your nocturnal clenching. It is simple to maintain by brushing with mild soap and lukewarm water.

Will a night guard feel uncomfortable while sleeping?

Because our night guards are custom molded to your unique arch, they are significantly slimmer than over-the-counter guards. Most patients adapt within 2 to 3 nights and experience immediate relief from morning tension.
