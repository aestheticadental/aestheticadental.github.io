Step-by-Step Guide

How to Take Good Dental Photos with Your Phone

You don't need professional camera gear. Follow these 5 simple shots in a well-lit room:

1

Full Face Smile

Stand facing a window or bright light. Smile naturally with your lips parted to show how your teeth frame your face.

2

Front Teeth in Bite

Pull your lips back, bite down naturally on your back molars, and take a close-up photo of your front teeth meeting.

3

Upper Arch (Roof of Mouth)

Tilt your head back, open wide, and angle the camera upward to capture all upper chewing surfaces and palate.

4

Lower Arch (Lower Teeth)

Tilt your chin down, open wide, and angle the camera downward to show the biting surfaces of all lower molars.

5

Close-Up of Problem Area

Zoom in cleanly on any specific broken tooth, dark filling, gap, or swollen gum area causing concern.

💡 Practical Tips for Clear Smartphone Shots:

• Turn Flash ON: The camera flash illuminates the dark corners of your mouth and highlights tooth translucency.
• Ask a Friend or Use Clean Spoons: Having someone else take the photos makes focusing easier. Alternatively, two clean teaspoons can serve as gentle cheek retractors to keep lips away from the teeth.
• Clean the Lens: Wipe your smartphone lens to eliminate smudges or blurriness.

⚖️ How to Request Existing X-Rays from Your Dentist in the US, UK, or Canada

Patients often hesitate to ask their local dentists for records. Remember: you legally own your dental records. Under HIPAA (US) and GDPR / Data Protection Acts (UK, Europe, Australia), your dentist is legally obligated to provide your digital X-rays upon request.

"Hello, I am traveling abroad and require copies of my dental records for continuity of care. Please email my latest digital OPG X-ray, periapical films, and 3D CBCT DICOM files in JPEG/ZIP format to this email address. Thank you."

Most clinics will email the digital attachments within 24 to 48 hours free of charge or for a nominal administrative fee.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Sending Dental Records

Clear answers to ensure smooth communication before you travel.

Do I have a legal right to request dental X-rays from my dentist in the US or UK? +

Yes, absolutely. Under health privacy laws such as HIPAA in the United States and GDPR / Data Protection Act in the UK and European Union, patients have a legal right to receive copies of their medical and dental records. You can simply email your local dental office and request your digital X-rays (OPG, IOPA) and CBCT scans sent in JPEG, PNG, or DICOM format. Clinics routinely email them within 24 to 48 hours.

Can I get an accurate cost estimate if I do not have recent X-rays? +

If you don't have recent X-rays, you can send 5 clear smartphone photos of your teeth and a description of your primary concerns. Dr. Prachi can provide an initial estimated cost range. However, for bone-level assessments (dental implants) or internal root infections (root canals), an X-ray or CBCT scan is essential for a final, fixed-price quote. If needed, we can take instant digital RVG X-rays at our clinic upon arrival for ₹200–₹300.

How should I send heavy 3D CBCT scans (DICOM files)? +

CBCT scans typically consist of a folder containing hundreds of raw DICOM (.dcm) slice files, usually 100MB to 500MB in size. Compress (ZIP) the entire CBCT folder and upload it to Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, or WeTransfer. Then share the download link with us on WhatsApp (+91 92266 80164) or via email at info@aestheticadental.in.

Why is my medical history important for a dental procedure? +

Systemic health significantly affects oral healing. For example, unmanaged diabetes (HbA1c above 7.5%) can impair implant osseointegration; blood thinners (Aspirin, Warfarin, Clopidogrel) require specific surgical bleeding management; and osteoporosis medications (bisphosphonates) require precautions during extractions. Disclosing your medical history ensures safe, predictable clinical outcomes.

How long does it take to receive a treatment proposal after sending records? +

Once we receive your dental records and photos on WhatsApp, Dr. Prachi Gupta Garg personally reviews your case and provides an itemized treatment proposal within 24 to 48 hours. This includes transparent costs in INR and foreign currency (USD/GBP), procedural timelines, and recommended appointment dates.
