Aligners vs Braces — Which Is Right for You?

Both options are effective. Dr. Prachi will recommend the best choice based on your alignment needs, lifestyle and budget.

😶

Clear Aligners — Nearly Invisible

Custom-made transparent plastic trays that fit snugly over your teeth. Nearly undetectable when worn. Ideal for working professionals and adults who want to straighten teeth without anyone noticing. Removable for eating, drinking, and brushing.

🔩

Metal Braces — Most Effective

Traditional brackets and wires bonded to teeth. The most reliable option for complex alignment issues, severe crowding, and bite corrections (overbite, underbite, crossbite). Works 24/7 — no compliance required from the patient.

⬜

Ceramic Braces — Less Visible

Same principle as metal braces but with tooth-coloured or clear ceramic brackets that blend with your teeth. A middle ground between the effectiveness of braces and the aesthetics of aligners.

Clear Aligner Treatment Process

1
Free Consultation

Dr. Prachi assesses your teeth, bite and alignment. X-rays or digital scans are taken. Suitability for aligners is confirmed.

2
Digital Smile Plan

A digital treatment plan is created showing the step-by-step movement of your teeth and the projected final result.

3
Custom Tray Fabrication

Your personalised set of aligner trays is fabricated based on the digital scan. Each tray represents one step in your treatment.

4
Wearing Your Aligners

Wear each tray for 20–22 hours per day. Switch to the next tray every 1–2 weeks as directed by Dr. Prachi.

5
Progress Check-ups

Regular check-ups every 6–8 weeks to monitor progress and ensure teeth are moving as planned.

6
Retainer Phase

After treatment, a retainer is prescribed to hold teeth in their new position — an essential step to maintain your results long-term.

Aligner FAQs

How much do clear aligners cost in Punawale?

Clear aligner treatment at Aesthetica costs ₹60,000 – ₹1,20,000 for a full course (both arches). Simpler single-arch cases start from ₹30,000. A detailed cost plan is provided before treatment begins.

How long does clear aligner treatment take?

Treatment duration is typically 6–18 months depending on complexity. Mild alignment cases may complete in 6 months; more complex cases take longer. You'll see gradual improvement from your first few trays.

Are clear aligners better than traditional braces?

Clear aligners are better for aesthetics, comfort and oral hygiene. Traditional braces are better for complex cases and patients who may not wear aligners consistently. Dr. Prachi will assess your teeth and recommend the most suitable option.

How many hours a day must I wear aligners?

For best results, aligners should be worn for 20–22 hours per day. They should only be removed for eating, drinking (anything other than water), and brushing.

Can adults get clear aligner treatment?

Absolutely. Clear aligners are especially popular with adults because they are nearly invisible and fit easily into professional life. There is no age limit — as long as teeth and gums are healthy, adults of any age can undergo aligner treatment.

Is aligner treatment available for teenagers?

Yes. Clear aligners can be used for teenagers once most permanent teeth have erupted (typically from age 13–14). Dr. Prachi will assess suitability at the consultation. For younger children, early orthodontic assessment from age 7–8 is recommended.
