Our Ethos

Our Guiding Mission & Core Values

We believe that dental appointments should be transparent, gentle, and centered around biological tooth preservation.

🦷

Preserving Natural Smiles

Natural teeth are irreplaceable biological assets. Our clinical approach prioritizes conservative treatments—using rotary endodontics, biomimetic fillings, and preventive care to save teeth rather than performing premature extractions.

🛡️

Transparent & Ethical Dentistry

We do not recommend procedures you don't need. Following an oral examination and digital X-ray review, Dr. Prachi outlines an itemized, clear treatment plan detailing exact steps and costs before any procedure begins.

🕊️

Anxiety-Free Patient Comfort

Dental anxiety affects patients of all ages. Through gentle local anesthesia, step-by-step procedure explanations, and unhurried appointments, we make sure every visit is painless and reassuring.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Aesthetica Dental Clinic

Everything you need to know about our practice, appointments, and sterilization protocols.

What makes Aesthetica Dental Clinic different from other clinics in Punawale? +

We operate on a conservative, tooth-preservation philosophy. We never recommend unnecessary procedures. Our clinic is equipped with advanced rotary endodontics, ultra-low radiation digital X-rays, Class-B autoclave hospital-grade sterilization, and provides transparent itemized pricing with free dedicated parking.

What infection control and sterilization standards do you follow? +

Patient safety is our top priority. We employ a multi-step sterilization protocol using high-temperature Class-B autoclaves, ultrasonic instrument baths, medical-grade pouch sealing, and 100% single-use disposable kits for diagnostic consultations.

Is the clinic easily accessible with parking? +

Yes. Aesthetica Dental Clinic is located on the 1st Floor (Shop No 103) of Swaraaj Heights on Kate Wasti Road, directly opposite Legacy Ivy. We provide free on-site parking for cars and two-wheelers, along with full elevator access.

Are you open on weekends and late evenings? +

Yes, we are open all 7 days of the week, including Sundays, from 10:15 AM to 8:30 PM. This schedule accommodates working professionals from Hinjawadi and Wakad as well as local families.
