What Is Cosmetic Gum Depigmentation?

Many individuals have dark brown or purplish patches on their gums caused by natural melanin hyperpigmentation. While medically harmless, dark gums can make some patients feel self-conscious when smiling. With our advanced soft-tissue diode dental laser, Dr. Prachi gently removes excess melanin granules, revealing natural, healthy coral-pink gums in a single appointment.

No Scalpels or Sutures

The micro-focused laser beam vaporizes pigmented tissue layers with micron precision without requiring surgical incisions, blades, or uncomfortable stitches.

Instant Coagulation & Zero Bleeding

The laser energy seals blood vessels and nerve endings instantly as it works, ensuring a virtually bloodless procedure with minimal post-operative soreness.

Rapid 5-Day Re-epithelialization

Because surrounding healthy tissues remain untouched by thermal damage, the epithelial layer regenerates smoothly within 5 to 7 days.

  • Laser-Assisted Periodontal Pocket Decontamination: Eliminating deep subgingival bacteria during deep dental scaling.

What to Expect During Your Laser Visit

Before beginning, a strong topical numbing gel or mild local anesthetic is applied. You and Dr. Prachi will wear protective laser safety glasses. The procedure typically takes only 20 to 30 minutes for the entire upper and lower aesthetic smile zone. You can return to work the same day with mild dietary precautions.
