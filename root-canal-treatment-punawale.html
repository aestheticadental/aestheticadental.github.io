What is a Single-Sitting Root Canal Treatment?

A single-sitting root canal treatment (RCT) is an advanced endodontic procedure where cleaning, disinfecting, and hermetically sealing an infected dental pulp is completed in one appointment under local anesthesia. Utilizing high-torque rotary instruments and computerized apex locators, single-visit RCT prevents bacterial recontamination between appointments, eliminates the need for multiple numbing shots, and relieves acute toothache immediately while preserving the natural tooth structure.

Signs & Symptoms

Do You Need a Root Canal? Common Warning Signs

When tooth decay penetrates the outer enamel and dentin to reach the living dental pulp, prompt clinical intervention is essential to prevent abscess formation and jawbone damage.

⚡

Severe, Spontaneous Pain

Continuous throbbing toothache that worsens when lying down at night, keeping you awake or radiating into your jaw, ear, or head.

☕

Lingering Thermal Sensitivity

Sharp, aching discomfort that persists for minutes after consuming hot tea, coffee, soup, ice cream, or cold water.

💥

Pain While Chewing or Biting

Tenderness or sharp discomfort when pressure is applied to the tooth while eating, indicating inflammation in the periodontal ligament.

🔴

Pimple or Swelling on Gums

A small gum boil (fistula) or facial swelling near the tooth root that releases a bitter fluid or causes noticeable tenderness.

Common Questions About Root Canal Treatments

Direct, clinical answers regarding RCT procedures, safety, and recovery in Punawale.

Is a single-sitting root canal treatment painful? +

No. Modern root canal treatment is virtually painless. Dr. Prachi Gupta Garg administers targeted local anesthesia before the procedure begins, completely numbing the tooth and surrounding gums. Most patients report feeling immediate relief from the throbbing toothache that brought them in.

How much does a Root Canal Treatment (RCT) cost in Punawale? +

At Aesthetica Dental Clinic in Punawale, root canal treatment typically starts from ₹3,500, depending on tooth location (front incisor vs. multi-rooted molar), infection severity, and whether it is a primary treatment or a re-RCT. We provide complete upfront pricing after a digital X-ray diagnosis.

Is a dental crown (cap) always necessary after an RCT? +

Yes, especially for premolars and molars that bear heavy chewing loads. Removing the infected pulp deprives the tooth of internal hydration, making it brittle over time. A custom metal-free zirconia or ceramic crown encapsulates the tooth, restoring full bite strength and preventing fractures.

Who is eligible for single-sitting root canal therapy? +

Most acute pulpitis cases, deep cavities without severe bone loss, fractured front teeth, and vital pulp exposures are ideal candidates for single-visit RCT. If severe pus drainage or extensive periapical abscess is present, Dr. Prachi may place an antibacterial dressing and complete the sealing in two visits.
