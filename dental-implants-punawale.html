Why Choose Dental Implants?

The gold standard for tooth replacement — implants are the only option that replaces both the root and the crown of a missing tooth.

🏆

Looks & Feels Like a Real Tooth

Custom-made crowns are shade-matched to your natural teeth using digital colour matching. No one will know it's an implant — it looks, feels and functions exactly like a natural tooth.

♾️

Lasts a Lifetime

Unlike dentures or bridges that need replacement every 5–10 years, a well-placed dental implant fuses permanently with your jawbone and can last a lifetime with regular dental care.

🦴

Prevents Bone Loss

When a tooth is lost, the jawbone in that area begins to deteriorate. A titanium implant acts as a tooth root, stimulating the bone and preventing the bone loss that causes a sunken facial appearance.

😊

No Harm to Adjacent Teeth

Unlike a dental bridge, implants don't require grinding down healthy neighbouring teeth. The implant stands independently in the jawbone, keeping your other teeth intact.

🍽️

Eat Without Restrictions

Implants restore full chewing strength — you can eat anything you like including hard and crunchy foods, unlike dentures which can slip or limit your diet.

🪥

Easy to Maintain

Brush and floss an implant exactly like a natural tooth. No adhesives, no soaking overnight, no special products — just normal oral hygiene habits.

The Dental Implant Process at Aesthetica

A carefully planned, phased process that ensures your implant fuses perfectly and lasts for decades.

1
Consultation & OPG X-Ray

Dr. Prachi assesses bone density and gum health using a digital OPG X-ray to confirm you are a suitable candidate for implants.

2
Treatment Planning

A personalised implant plan is created including implant position, crown design, and a full cost breakdown — before any procedure begins.

3
Implant Placement

The titanium implant post is placed into the jawbone under local anaesthesia. The procedure takes 45–90 minutes per implant.

4
Healing & Osseointegration

Over 3–4 months the implant fuses with the jawbone (osseointegration). A temporary crown may be placed during this period.

5
Abutment Placement

A small connector piece (abutment) is attached to the implant post to support the final crown.

6
Final Crown Fitting

A custom-crafted, shade-matched crown is permanently fixed onto the abutment — completing your new tooth.

Dental Implant FAQs

How much do dental implants cost in Punawale?

Dental implants at Aesthetica cost ₹25,000 – ₹45,000 per tooth (implant + crown). Cost varies based on bone density, the implant brand used, and the crown material. We provide a written cost breakdown at your consultation before any procedure begins.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The total process takes 3–6 months. The implant post is placed first and needs 3–4 months to fuse with the jawbone (osseointegration). The final crown is then fitted in a subsequent appointment.

Are dental implants painful?

The implant placement is done under local anaesthesia — you will not feel pain during the procedure. Mild soreness and swelling for 2–4 days after is normal and manageable with standard pain relief. Most patients return to normal activity the next day.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper oral hygiene and regular dental check-ups, dental implants can last a lifetime. The crown may need replacement after 10–15 years depending on wear. Implants have the highest long-term success rate of any tooth replacement option.

Is everyone suitable for dental implants?

Most healthy adults are suitable for dental implants. Sufficient jawbone density is required for the implant to fuse properly. If bone loss has occurred, a bone graft may be needed first. Dr. Prachi will assess your suitability with a digital X-ray at your consultation.

Implants vs bridge vs dentures — which is best?

Implants are the superior long-term solution — they don't damage adjacent teeth (unlike bridges), they don't slip (unlike dentures), and they preserve jawbone. While the upfront cost is higher, implants are the most cost-effective option over a 10–20 year period.
