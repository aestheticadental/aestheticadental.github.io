Clinical Transparency

What Factors Determine Your Final Treatment Cost?

Dental pricing varies based on individual biological and structural needs. Here is how Dr. Prachi evaluates your case:

🦷

Tooth Position & Anatomy

Front incisors have a single straight root canal, making RCT simpler. Molars have 3 to 4 curved canals requiring specialized rotary shaping and extra chair time.

🔬

Material Selection & Brand

Whether you choose porcelain-fused-to-metal (PFM) or metal-free monolithic CAD/CAM Zirconia crowns affects longevity, aesthetic translucency, and warranty.

⚠️

Severity of Infection

A routine cavity filling is quick and inexpensive. Deep pulp infections with bone abscesses require medication, apex locator verification, and possibly core build-ups.

🛡️

Hospital-Grade Sterilization

We use strict Class-B autoclave sterilization and single-use disposable kits to guarantee 100% patient cross-infection protection on every visit.
