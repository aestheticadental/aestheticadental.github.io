Hygiene standards vary widely across unaccredited clinics. Verify that your dental center utilizes medical-grade Class-B vacuum autoclaves, biological spore monitoring, and factory-sealed disposable patient exam kits opened in front of you.

Clear Flight & Post-Op Guidelines

Understand the recovery window. While routine cleanings and fillings allow immediate travel, minor surgeries, tooth extractions, and implant placements require adequate rest before flying due to aircraft cabin pressure changes.

✈️ Essential Flying Safety: When Can You Board a Plane After Dental Work?

Commercial aircraft cabins are pressurized to altitudes between 6,000 and 8,000 feet. This slight drop in atmospheric pressure causes trapped air pockets to expand, which can cause discomfort if proper clinical recovery time isn't observed.

Routine Fillings & Cleanings Safe to fly immediately or the next day. No pressure-related restrictions.
Single-Sitting Root Canals Safe to fly within 24 hours once the root canals are permanently obturated and sealed.
Simple Extractions & Implants Wait 48 to 72 hours to allow a secure blood clot to form and avoid pressure-induced bleeding.
Sinus Lifts & Major Bone Grafts Wait 7 to 10 days before long-haul international flights to protect delicate sinus membranes.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions by International Patients

Clear guidance on clinical safety, payment options, and warranties.

Is dental care in India safe and comparable to Western standards? +

Yes. Private dental clinics in India follow international clinical benchmarks. Premier clinics employ Class-B vacuum autoclave sterilization, digital low-radiation radiography (RVG), and exclusively use FDA- and CE-certified materials such as Straumann, Osstem, 3M, and Ivoclar Vivadent. Most dental surgeons are fluent in English, ensuring clear communication.

How long after dental surgery or tooth extraction is it safe to fly? +

For routine procedures such as teeth cleanings, fillings, single-sitting root canals, and crown fittings, you can safely fly the next day or even the same day. Following minor surgical extractions or standard implant fixture placements, waiting 48 to 72 hours is recommended to prevent sinus or blood pressure fluctuations inside pressurized aircraft cabins. For complex sinus lifts or bone grafting, a rest window of 7 to 10 days before long-haul flights is advisable.

How do dental warranties work for patients traveling back home? +

Reputable clinics issue international manufacturer warranty cards for dental implants and CAD/CAM Zirconia crowns. These cards include unique component serial numbers and authenticity QR codes. Global implant systems (such as Straumann or Osstem) have worldwide component compatibility, meaning any certified dentist in your home country can access matching prosthetic parts if future maintenance is needed.

What payment methods are accepted for international dental care? +

At Aesthetica Dental Clinic, international patients can pay using major international credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), international bank wire transfers, local cash (INR), or digital UPI apps if using an Indian bank account.
