Clear Aligner Daily Care & Maintenance Protocol

Essential guidelines for tray hygiene, the 22-hour wear rule, aligner chewies usage, and keeping your orthodontic treatment on track with Dr. Prachi Gupta Garg.

Ask on WhatsApp

The Golden Rule: 20 to 22 Hours of Daily Wear

Unlike traditional metal braces that remain bonded 24/7, clear aligners depend directly on your personal commitment. Trays should only be removed for meals, drinking anything other than plain water, and brushing your teeth.

If you fail to wear your aligners for the prescribed 22 hours daily, your teeth will not complete the micro-movements programmed for that specific tray, resulting in "tracking lag" and ill-fitting future aligners.

How to Clean Your Trays Without Damaging Them

DO: Brush with Mild Clear Liquid Soap

Every morning and evening, gently brush the inside and outside of your trays with a soft toothbrush and lukewarm water with a drop of gentle liquid soap.

DO NOT: Use Toothpaste on Trays

Most commercial toothpastes contain abrasive silica particles designed to polish enamel. These abrasives will scratch medical-grade polyurethane, making your clear trays appear cloudy and hazy.

DO NOT: Use Hot Water

Never soak or rinse your aligners in hot or boiling water. Heat softens thermo-plastic polymer, warping tray dimensions and ruining programmed tooth movements.

Why You Must Use Aligner Chewies

Aligner chewies are small cylindrical foam rolls provided in your starter kit. Biting down firmly on a chewie for 5 to 10 minutes every time you insert your trays (especially right before bedtime) eliminates air pockets between the tray plastic and your teeth. This ensures the biomechanical force vectors transfer accurately to the root.

Switching to Your Next Set of Trays

  1. Switch Trays at Night: Insert your new set of trays right before sleeping. This allows the initial tight pressure to settle while you rest, minimizing daytime discomfort.
  2. Keep Your Previous Trays: Always store your immediate previous set in their protective pouch. If you ever misplace your current set, your previous set prevents teeth from relapsing while a replacement is fabricated.
  3. Never Throw Old Trays in Napkins: The #1 way patients lose aligners is by wrapping them in restaurant paper napkins during lunch. Always place them directly into your hard protective travel case.
