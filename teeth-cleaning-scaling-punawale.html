Why Professional Teeth Cleaning Is Essential

Brushing and flossing maintain your teeth — but professional cleaning protects them at a deeper level.

🦠

Removes Tartar You Can't Brush Away

Plaque that is not removed within 24–72 hours hardens into tartar (calculus) — a hard mineral deposit that no amount of brushing can remove. Only professional scaling instruments can safely remove tartar without damaging teeth or gums.

🦷

Prevents Gum Disease

Tartar below the gumline triggers gingivitis (gum inflammation) which, if untreated, progresses to periodontitis — a serious infection that destroys the bone supporting your teeth. Regular scaling is the most effective way to prevent this progression.

😮

Eliminates Bad Breath

The majority of cases of persistent bad breath (halitosis) originate from bacteria in tartar deposits and infected gum pockets. Professional cleaning eliminates these deposits and the bacteria causing the odour — often instantly and dramatically.

✨

Removes Surface Stains

The polishing step after scaling removes surface stains from tea, coffee, tobacco and food — leaving teeth noticeably cleaner and brighter. While not a substitute for professional whitening, polishing significantly improves the appearance of natural teeth.

🔍

Early Detection of Problems

Every scaling appointment includes a clinical check-up. Early detection of cavities, cracked teeth, oral cancer signs and gum disease means simpler, less expensive treatment. Many serious conditions are caught at routine clean appointments.

💰

Saves Money Long-Term

A ₹800–₹1,500 professional clean every 6 months prevents the far more costly treatments that untreated gum disease requires — root planing, gum surgery, or eventual tooth replacement with implants. Prevention is always cheaper than cure.

Types of Professional Dental Cleaning at Aesthetica

The right type of cleaning depends on your current oral health status — assessed at every visit.

1️⃣
Routine Scaling & Polishing (Prophylaxis)
For patients with healthy gums and mild-to-moderate tartar buildup. Ultrasonic scaling removes deposits above and just below the gumline, followed by polishing to remove stains. Duration: 30–45 minutes. Recommended every 6 months. Cost: ₹800–₹1,500.
2️⃣
Deep Cleaning — Scaling & Root Planing (SRP)
For patients with gum disease (periodontitis) and deep periodontal pockets. Removes tartar and bacterial deposits from below the gumline and smooths root surfaces to prevent reattachment of bacteria. Done under local anaesthesia. Cost: ₹2,000–₹6,000 (per quadrant).
3️⃣
Airflow / Stain Removal Polishing
Uses a controlled jet of fine powder and water to remove stubborn surface stains from coffee, tea, tobacco and plaque from difficult-to-reach areas. Particularly effective as preparation before professional teeth whitening. Cost: ₹1,000–₹2,000.
4️⃣
Periodontal Maintenance
For patients who have had gum disease treatment and need more frequent professional cleaning (every 3–4 months) to maintain results and prevent recurrence. Includes pocket-depth monitoring and assessment of gum health.

Teeth Cleaning Process at Aesthetica

A thorough, comfortable cleaning completed in one appointment.

1
Clinical Examination

Dr. Prachi examines your teeth, gums and existing restorations. Pocket depths are measured to assess gum health. X-rays taken if needed.

2
Ultrasonic Scaling

An ultrasonic scaler uses high-frequency vibrations and a water spray to remove tartar deposits above and below the gumline — quickly and without discomfort.

3
Hand Scaling

Fine hand instruments are used to remove any remaining deposits in tight spaces between teeth and in gum pockets — areas the ultrasonic scaler may not reach.

4
Polishing

A rotating polishing cup with prophylaxis paste smooths tooth surfaces and removes surface stains from tea, coffee and tobacco — leaving teeth clean and bright.

5
Flossing & Irrigation

Spaces between teeth are cleaned and irrigated with antibacterial solution if needed — especially important for patients with gum pockets or bleeding gums.

6
Oral Hygiene Advice

Personalised brushing technique, correct flossing method, and diet advice are provided at every visit — empowering you to maintain results between appointments.

Teeth Cleaning FAQs

How much does teeth cleaning cost in Punawale?

Routine teeth cleaning (scaling and polishing) at Aesthetica Dental Clinic in Punawale costs ₹800 – ₹1,500 for a full-mouth clean. Deep cleaning (scaling and root planing) for gum disease costs ₹2,000 – ₹3,500 per quadrant, depending on severity.

Is teeth scaling painful?

Routine scaling at Aesthetica is generally comfortable and performed without anaesthesia. Patients with sensitive gums or heavy tartar may feel mild discomfort — in these cases, numbing gel or local anaesthesia can be applied first to ensure a pain-free experience.

How often should I get professional teeth cleaning?

Every 6 months is recommended for most patients. Those with a history of gum disease, heavy tartar buildup, smoking or diabetes may need cleaning every 3–4 months. Dr. Prachi will advise the right frequency for your situation.

Does scaling damage teeth or make them sensitive?

No. Professional scaling does not damage enamel or weaken teeth. Some patients experience mild sensitivity for 1–3 days after deep cleaning as gums recede from heavy deposits — this is normal and temporary. Using a sensitivity toothpaste for a few days helps.

Will cleaning remove stains from tea and coffee?

Yes. The polishing step removes most surface stains from tea, coffee, tobacco and food, leaving teeth noticeably cleaner and brighter. For deeper intrinsic staining, professional teeth whitening after scaling delivers even more dramatic results.

Can I get scaling done if I have gum disease or bleeding gums?

Yes — in fact, scaling is the primary treatment for gum disease and bleeding gums. Bleeding gums are a sign of gingivitis caused by bacterial buildup. Scaling removes the cause, and gum bleeding typically reduces significantly within 2–4 weeks of thorough cleaning.
