Quick Questions

Do I need an appointment or can I walk in?

Both are welcome. Walk-ins are accepted subject to availability. Appointments are preferred to minimise waiting time — call +91 92266 80164 or WhatsApp us to book your slot.

Are you open on Sundays and public holidays?

Yes. Aesthetica Dental Clinic is open every day of the week — Monday through Sunday, including public holidays — from 10:15 AM to 8:30 PM.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Debit Cards and Credit Cards. EMI is available on select treatments such as dental implants and clear aligners.

How far is the clinic from Wakad and Hinjewadi?

Aesthetica Dental Clinic is approximately 10–15 minutes from Wakad and 15–20 minutes from Hinjewadi IT Park (Phases 1, 2 & 3) by road.
