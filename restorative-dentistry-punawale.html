A full range of restorative solutions — from simple fillings to full-mouth rehabilitation — tailored to your needs and budget.

Tooth-Coloured Composite Fillings

The modern standard for cavity treatment. Composite resin is bonded directly to the tooth in a single appointment, matching your natural tooth colour perfectly. No silver amalgam — only invisible, aesthetic fillings that restore full function and blend seamlessly. From ₹800 per tooth.

Dental Crowns (Caps)

Full coverage restoration for teeth that are severely damaged, cracked, or have undergone root canal treatment. Available in zirconia (strongest, most aesthetic), ceramic, and porcelain-fused-to-metal. Digital shade-matching ensures a completely natural appearance. From ₹5,000 per crown.

Dental Bridges

A fixed solution for replacing one or more missing teeth where implants are not suitable or desired. A bridge spans the gap using adjacent teeth as supports, with an artificial tooth (pontic) suspended between. Restores chewing function and prevents teeth from drifting. From ₹12,000 for a 3-unit bridge.

Inlays & Onlays

Custom-made restorations fabricated in a dental lab for teeth with moderate-to-large cavities that are too damaged for a simple filling but don't yet need a full crown. Stronger and more durable than composite fillings, with a precise fit. Ceramic inlays are tooth-coloured and virtually invisible. From ₹5,000 per tooth.

🏗️

Full Mouth Rehabilitation

A comprehensive treatment plan combining multiple restorative procedures — fillings, crowns, root canals, implants and more — to restore the entire mouth to health, full function and an aesthetic appearance. Tailored to severely worn, damaged or neglected dentitions. Custom quotes provided.

🦷

Post & Core Build-Up

When a tooth has lost significant structure after decay or fracture, a post and core build-up creates the foundation needed to support a crown. A fibre post is placed into the root canal space and a core of composite builds up the tooth to the shape required for crown placement.

When Do You Need Restorative Dentistry?

Don't wait for pain to visit a dentist — many restorative issues are best treated early, before they escalate.

🕳️
Cavities / Tooth Decay
A cavity caught early needs only a small filling. Left untreated, decay reaches the nerve — requiring root canal treatment, a crown, and significantly higher cost. Regular 6-monthly check-ups catch cavities before they hurt.
💥
Chipped or Cracked Teeth
A chip or crack in the enamel, if left untreated, can propagate deeper with chewing forces — eventually splitting the tooth or allowing bacteria to reach the pulp. Composite bonding or a crown stops the damage at its current stage.
🦷
Broken or Missing Fillings
Old fillings can crack, fall out or leak — exposing the underlying tooth to bacteria and decay. A replacement filling restores the seal. If the remaining tooth is too compromised, an inlay or crown may be needed.
😶
Missing Teeth
Missing teeth cause neighbouring teeth to drift, affect bite function, and accelerate bone loss in the jaw. A bridge or implant-supported crown restores function, prevents drift, and maintains jawbone volume.
🪨
Worn-Down Teeth
Teeth worn down by grinding (bruxism), acid erosion, or abrasion lose enamel progressively. Crowns, onlays or composite additions restore lost tooth height and protect remaining enamel from further wear.
⚡
After Root Canal Treatment
Every tooth that has undergone root canal treatment should receive a crown — especially back teeth. Without a crown, the brittle, non-vital tooth is prone to fracture under chewing forces. A crown is the final protective step.

Restorative Dentistry FAQs

How much does a tooth filling cost in Punawale?

Tooth-coloured composite fillings at Aesthetica cost ₹800 – ₹2,500 per tooth depending on cavity size and complexity. Small single-surface fillings cost less; large multi-surface fillings affecting most of the tooth cost more. All fillings at Aesthetica are tooth-coloured — no silver amalgam.

Are tooth-coloured fillings better than silver amalgam?

Yes — composite (tooth-coloured) fillings bond directly to the tooth, preserving more natural tooth structure and blending invisibly. They contain no mercury and are both aesthetically and clinically superior for most cavity sizes. Aesthetica uses only composite fillings.

How long do dental fillings last?

Composite fillings typically last 7–10 years with good oral hygiene. Large fillings in high-stress areas may need replacement sooner. Regular 6-monthly check-ups allow us to monitor fillings and replace them before they fail, preventing larger problems.

What is the difference between an inlay and a filling?

A filling is placed directly in the tooth at the appointment. An inlay is custom-fabricated in a dental laboratory and bonded into the tooth in a second appointment. Inlays are stronger, more precise and longer-lasting than direct fillings — used when a cavity is too large for a reliable filling but does not require a full crown.

Is a bridge or an implant better for a missing tooth?

Implants are the superior long-term solution — they preserve jawbone and don't require preparation of adjacent teeth. Bridges are a faster and more affordable option where implants are not suitable (insufficient bone, medical reasons, or patient preference). Dr. Prachi will recommend the best option after assessing your case.

Do I need a crown after root canal treatment?

Yes — particularly for back teeth (molars and premolars). Root canal treatment removes the tooth's nerve and blood supply, making it more brittle over time. A crown distributes chewing forces across the tooth and prevents fracture — which could make the tooth non-restorable. It is the essential final step after RCT.
