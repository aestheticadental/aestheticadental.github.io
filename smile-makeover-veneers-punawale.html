What Is a Digital Smile Makeover?

A smile makeover harmonizes the proportions, shade, and alignment of your front teeth to complement your facial structure, lip line, and skin tone. Dr. Prachi combines her formal dental surgical background with advanced training in clinical cosmetology to deliver natural, radiant smiles without looking artificial or overly bulky.

Cosmetic Dental Concerns We Correct:

  • Midline Gaps (Diastema): Unwanted spaces between front teeth closed seamlessly.
  • Deep Enamel Stains: Tetracycline or fluorosis discoloration resistant to regular teeth whitening.
  • Chipped, Fractured or Uneven Teeth: Restoring symmetrical biting edges.
  • Peg Laterals & Microdontia: Resizing undersized teeth into balanced alignment.
  • Worn Biting Edges: Rebuilding teeth shortened by years of clenching or erosion.

Our Step-by-Step Smile Design Workflow

  1. Aesthetic Consultation & Photography: High-resolution digital facial photos, smile analysis, and shade assessment.
  2. Wax-Up & Mockup Preview: You get to preview and approve the proposed tooth shape and smile curve before any preparation begins.
  3. Conservative Micro-Preparation: Gentle enamel shaping under local anesthesia to ensure an ultra-flush gumline margin.
  4. Precision Digital Impressions: Sent to a master ceramic laboratory for individual characterization and optical translucency.
  5. Adhesive Bonding: Permanent resin cementation with dual-cure bonding systems for immense structural strength.

Combining Aligners & Veneers

When teeth are severely rotated or crowded, Dr. Prachi often recommends short-term pre-aligner therapy with clear aligners to reposition teeth first. This eliminates the need for excessive enamel shaving, ensuring your veneers are as conservative as possible.
